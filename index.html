Privacy Policy — Bohni

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

The short version

Bohni reads the payment notifications your phone already shows you, records each payment, reads it aloud, and lets you issue bills. Everything it records stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server holding your payments. We cannot see your transactions, because they are never sent to us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your payment amounts, the names of people who pay you, reference numbers, or the text of any notification.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • There is no user account. Nothing to register, nothing to sign in to.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. There is no data to sell.
  • We show no advertisements and do not request an advertising identifier.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Notification access, and why the app asks for it

Reading payment notifications is the app's entire purpose. Android grants this through notification access, which you turn on yourself and can turn off at any time in your phone's Settings.

  • What is read: payment notifications from UPI apps and bank apps — the amount, who paid or was paid, and the reference number.
  • One-time passwords and security codes are discarded. They are never stored, never shown in your records, and never read aloud. The app detects and rejects them before anything is saved.
  • Everything else is discarded. Android delivers every notification to any app with this permission; Bohni keeps only payments and drops the rest without storing them. The one exception is the same as for messages below: something that looks like a payment but cannot be read is kept on your phone for up to 30 days so the fault can be fixed, and is then deleted automatically.

Bank text messages

Many payments produce no notification from any app — a card payment, an ATM withdrawal, a bank transfer, a salary credit. For those, the text message your bank sends is the only record your phone receives. The app asks for permission to receive incoming messages so that those payments reach your records too.

  • It is optional. You can refuse, and the app works: it simply records fewer payments, and tells you so. Nothing is disabled.
  • Only new messages, as they arrive. The app holds RECEIVE_SMS and not READ_SMS, which is a real difference: it cannot open your message history, and cannot see a single message that arrived before you gave permission.
  • Only payments are kept. The amount, who paid or was paid, and the reference number are extracted on your phone, and the message itself is then discarded — except in the one case described below.
  • One-time passwords and security codes are detected and rejected before anything is saved. They are never stored, never shown and never read aloud.
  • Every other message is discarded without being stored, and none of it is ever sent anywhere.
  • One exception, stated plainly: if a message looks like a payment but the app cannot read it, the text is kept on your phone for up to 30 days so the fault can be found and fixed, then deleted automatically. It is never sent anywhere, and Settings → Clear All Data removes it immediately.
  • You can revoke this at any time in your phone's Settings.

What is stored, and where

All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Payments captured from notifications: amount, direction, counterparty name, reference number, time, and which app reported it.
  • Bills and receipts you create, including any customer name or phone number you type, and the items you sell.
  • Your shop details, if you enter them, and your app settings.

Your payments are deliberately excluded from Google backup. Most apps upload their data to your Google Drive automatically; Bohni switches that off for your payment records, so your takings are not copied to any cloud service. Your records do transfer directly to a new phone if you use Android's phone-to-phone transfer, because that is a local copy that never reaches a server.

One consequence is worth stating plainly: if you factory-reset your phone or uninstall the app without exporting first, your records are gone and we cannot recover them, because we never had them. Use Settings → Export data to keep your own copy.

What does leave your phone

Two things, neither of which contains payment information:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a technical report is sent so the fault can be fixed. It contains no amounts, no names and no notification text.
  • Anonymous usage counts (Firebase Analytics). Which screens are opened and how often the app runs, tied to a random identifier rather than to you. This is used to find where the app is confusing or broken. It contains no payment information and no advertising identifier.

Both can be switched off in Settings → Data & privacy. Turning them off stops the collection itself, not merely the sending of it.

Firebase is operated by Google; their handling of this diagnostic data is governed by the Firebase Privacy Policy.

Payment for the app

Recording payments is free, and stays free. A subscription is what reads payments aloud and lets you share bills as PDFs.

  • New shops get 30 days free. It needs no card and no payment details — nothing is entered until you decide to pay.
  • After that: ₹29 a month, or pay once for three months, six months or a year at a lower rate. A pass can be paid for with a Google Play gift card bought for cash, so you do not need a card or a bank-linked account.
  • If you are tracking your own money rather than running a shop, the app is free forever and there is nothing to buy.
  • Your records are never held hostage. Payments go on being recorded whatever you pay or do not pay, and export is free in every case.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive no card details and store no payment method; the app keeps only whether a subscription is currently active.

Other permissions

  • Notifications — to show you a payment alert.
  • Ignore battery optimisation (optional) — Android otherwise delays background apps, which would make a spoken payment alert arrive late or not at all. Payments are still recorded without it.
  • Text-to-speech — payments are read aloud using the speech engine already on your phone, in English, Hindi or one of ten other Indian languages. The sentence is built and spoken on the device; nothing is sent anywhere to say it.
  • Foreground service / media playback — recent Android versions mute audio started by a background app, so the spoken alert is played from a short-lived foreground service. It is used only to speak, never to record audio.
  • Internet — used only for crash reports and anonymous usage counts. The app captures and records payments with no internet connection at all.
  • Receive SMS (optional) — to read bank payment alerts as they arrive, as described above. Not your message history, which the app cannot open.
  • Billing — so a subscription can be bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees a card number.
  • No microphone, no camera, no contacts, no location.

Your control

  • Turn notification access off at any time in your phone's Settings; the app stops reading anything immediately.
  • Correct or delete any individual payment record from within the app.
  • Export everything as CSV, Excel or a backup file, at any time, free.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it has stored.

Children

Bohni is a business and personal finance tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect nothing from them — as above, we collect no personal information from anyone.

2. Accuracy, and what you must not rely on

The app reads notifications written by other companies, whose wording changes without notice. It may occasionally miss a payment, record one twice, or read a detail wrongly. Do not treat it as proof that money has arrived. Confirm the payment in your bank or UPI app before handing over goods. Records are provided for your own convenience and are not a substitute for your bank statement.

The app can produce GST tax invoices and plain receipts from details you enter. You are responsible for the accuracy of those details, for holding a valid GST registration before issuing a tax invoice, and for your own tax compliance.
